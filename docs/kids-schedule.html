Where the week pinches

Six places the schedule collides, and what I’d do about each.

  • Saturday AM

    Elle’s soccer and volleyball games are both Saturday morning. Volleyball starts November 9 and soccer runs “through November,” so for up to three weeks she is booked into two games at the same time — not just a busy day, an actual double-booking.

    This is the one thing on the board that can’t be solved by driving faster. Get the soccer end date, and if it overlaps, decide now which games she misses.

    Confirm the soccer end date
  • Sep – Nov

    All three kids have Saturday-morning soccer games. From mid-September, Elle, Callie and Davey are on three different fields at overlapping times.

    Plan two drivers for these ten weeks, and ask each coach for the game schedule the day it’s published.

    Needs two adults
  • Mon & Sun

    Your CCD plan works, and it’s only two switches. Monday CCD and Monday volleyball are both 4:30, so they can never share a week.

    With the season running Sept 20 – Apr 26: Mondays Sept 20 – Nov 8, Sundays Nov 9 – Jan 16, Mondays Jan 17 – Apr 26. Both switches land on a volleyball boundary.

    No change needed
  • Thursday

    LitWits is blocked until January, then free. It runs 3:45–4:45 against a 4:30 gymnastics start — but once dance takes over in January, gymnastics stops and Thursday afternoon empties out.

    So it isn’t a yes or no, it’s a when: sign Elle up for the spring session, not the fall one.

    Works from January
  • Tuesday

    Art Club fits now that Elle’s piano is at 4:30. With Callie at 4:00 and Elle behind her, the club’s 3:30–4:30 slot no longer overlaps a lesson — it just runs right up against one.

    Zero travel time between the two, so it only works if the club and the piano lesson are essentially in the same place.

    Check the travel time
  • Monday

    Spanish Club has the same zero-gap problem. It ends at 4:30 and whatever Elle has on Monday — volleyball or CCD — starts at 4:30.

    Of the three clubs, this is the one with the least slack, because it butts against something every single week of the year.

    Check the travel time
