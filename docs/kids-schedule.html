Where the week pinches

Five places the schedule still collides, worst first — plus one that just fixed itself.

  • November

    Elle’s soccer and volleyball games are both Saturday morning. Volleyball starts November 9 and soccer runs “through November,” so for up to three weeks she is booked into two games at the same time — a genuine double-booking, not just a full day.

    This is the one thing on the board that can’t be solved by driving faster. Get the soccer end date, and if it overlaps, decide now which games she misses.

    Confirm the soccer end date
  • Sep – Nov

    Elle and Callie both have Saturday-morning games. Two fields, overlapping times, from mid-September until the fall seasons end.

    Better than it looked before — Davey has no Saturday games at all, so he can ride along with either parent. Two drivers still covers it.

    Needs two adults
  • CCD

    The whole CCD plan assumes you can move between Sunday and Monday mid-year. Some programs make you register for one session and hold it all season.

    If they won’t let you switch, take Sunday for the full year. Sunday never collides with anything; Monday collides with volleyball for the ten weeks from November 9 to January 16.

    Ask before you register
  • Thursday

    Alternating LitWits with gymnastics works — but you only need it until January. The two overlap by 15 minutes, so on a LitWits month Elle sits out gymnastics. From January gymnastics stops for dance and Thursday afternoon is clear, so she can take LitWits every month with nothing in its way.

    One catch: Callie is at gymnastics at 4:30 either way, so a LitWits month doesn’t save you a trip — it adds a second pickup at 4:45. The zero-hassle version is to skip the fall entirely and take every month from January; alternate only if Elle wants to start now.

    Alternate Sept–Dec, or just wait for January
  • Mon & Tue

    Both remaining clubs end at 4:30, and Elle has something starting at 4:30 on both days. Spanish Club runs into volleyball or CCD; Art Club runs into her piano lesson.

    Neither actually overlaps — the piano swap fixed that — but neither leaves a minute of travel time. They only work if the club and the 4:30 activity are essentially in the same place.

    Check the travel time
  • Wednesday

    Solved by Davey’s move to 5:00. This was the worst thing on the board — two soccer fields at the same half hour, every week, most of the year. At 5:00–5:30 he now finishes a clear 30 minutes before Callie starts at 6:00.

    Different fields, but sequential: one driver takes Davey, then crosses town for Callie. The only thing to watch is whether 30 minutes is enough to get between the two — and if Callie’s is actually 6:30, you have a full hour.

    No longer a conflict
