Where the week pinches

Four places the schedule collides, and what I’d do about each.

  • Mon & Sun

    CCD follows volleyball — and it works. Your instinct was right. Monday CCD (4:30–5:30) and Monday volleyball practice (4:30) are the same hour, so they can never coexist.

    Sunday CCD Aug 26–Sep 5 and again Nov 9–Jan 16. Monday CCD Sep 6–Nov 8 and Jan 17 onward. That’s two switches a year, both on a clean season boundary.

    No change needed
  • Tuesday

    Art Club can’t coexist with piano at 4:00. The club runs 3:30–4:30 and Ellie’s lesson is at 4:00 — a straight 30-minute overlap, every week, all year.

    The cheap fix: move Ellie’s piano to about 5:00 and keep Callie at 4:30. You’d still make one trip, just with the girls in the other order. Otherwise Art Club is out.

    Move piano or drop Art Club
  • Thursday

    LitWits can’t coexist with gymnastics either. 3:45–4:45 against a 4:30 gymnastics start, and gymnastics is the one slot where both girls go at once — much more expensive to move.

    And from January, Thursday already carries gymnastics at 4:30 plus dance at 6:30. That’s the fullest evening of the week before LitWits is even in it.

    LitWits is the one to skip
  • Monday

    Spanish Club is the only club that fits — barely. It ends at 4:30 and whatever Ellie has on Monday (volleyball or CCD) starts at 4:30, so there’s zero travel time.

    Workable only if the club and the Monday activity are in the same building. If they aren’t, none of the three clubs fit without moving something.

    Check the walking distance
  • Saturday

    Mid-September to November is the crunch. Ellie, Callie and Davey all have soccer games on the same mornings, at three different fields, at times nobody has given you yet.

    This is the stretch to plan two drivers for, and the first thing to ask each coach about when schedules come out.

    Needs two adults
  • November

    Soccer and volleyball may overlap. Ellie’s soccer runs “through November” and volleyball starts November 9 — so up to three weeks where she’d be in both, with volleyball taking Monday, Wednesday and Saturday.

    Confirm the soccer end date
