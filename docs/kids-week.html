Filling the gaps, cheapest first

Four of the five moves below cost you no new trips at all, because they slot into things you are already driving to.

  • No new trip

    Callie and Davey join CCD on Sundays at 10:15. You are already dropping Elle there and coming back for Mass at 11:00 in the same building. Three children in the same hour, same building, zero extra driving — and it fills the one pillar Callie is missing and one of Davey’s three.

    The only question is whether Davey is old enough; most programmes start around first grade.

  • No new trip

    Davey takes the 5:00 piano slot on Tuesdays. Callie is at 4:00 and Elle at 4:30, so a third half-hour lesson runs to 5:30 and you make exactly the same single trip. That fills his instrument pillar for thirty extra minutes on a day you are already there.

  • A decision

    Elle picks one artistic outlet. She currently has three. Dance from January is the strongest candidate — it is the one that is genuinely hers rather than a school club, and Callie does it alongside her.

    That would make Art Club and LitWits the ones to let go, which also clears the last recurring squeeze on the board: the Tuesday Art-Club-into-piano scramble disappears entirely.

  • Two off the list

    T-ball and jujitsu come off. Davey’s soccer is year-round, so either would be a second competitive sport. Under this framework both are out — which closes two of your open questions without another phone call.

  • One new trip

    Davey needs an artistic outlet — the only genuinely new thing on this list. Thursday is his open afternoon and you are already out with the girls for gymnastics or dance, so an art or music class near either venue would ride along on a trip you are making anyway.

    Monday afternoons are also completely free outside volleyball weeks, if Thursday turns out to be too full.

Still to pin down

The blanks that would move something on this page. Every time on this page is now confirmed except where a row says otherwise. The first two are the ones that would still change the shape of the week.

  1. When does Elle’s fall soccer end? Volleyball starts Nov 9 and both play Saturday mornings — the one real double-booking on the board.
  2. Which night is Elle’s soccer practice? Both seasons. Pencilled on Wednesday, but it could land anywhere.
  3. Which Wednesdays are volleyball game nights? They need two drivers at 6:00 while Callie is in soccer — worth marking on a calendar as soon as the fixtures come out.
  4. Callie’s soccer start and end dates. Three things: her Wednesday practices, which night the in-laws dinner falls on, and when Wednesday goes back to one sitting.
  5. Elle and Callie’s Saturday game times. Needed before I can say whether one driver could ever cover the morning.
  6. What order do the tennis lessons go in? I have Davey first and Elle last so the youngest is not waiting until 5:00. Easy to flip.
  7. Is Callie’s Wednesday practice 6:00 or 6:30? 6:30 gives a full hour between the two fields, and lets her eat at 5:00 instead of 4:30.
  8. What time are the Wednesday volleyball games? The only weekday commitment I can’t place on the timeline.
  9. What date in January does dance start? It’s also the date gymnastics ends, so it moves two things at once.
  10. How old are the three of them? Decides whether Davey can start CCD and piano now, and how much he should reasonably carry.
  11. Which artistic outlet is Elle keeping? Dance, Art Club or LitWits. Dropping the two school clubs also clears the Tuesday piano squeeze.
